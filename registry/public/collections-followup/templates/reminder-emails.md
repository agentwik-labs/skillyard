# Reminder email templates

Replace every <placeholder>. Keep each message under 120 words.

## Before due (3 days before)
Subject: Invoice <number> due <date>
Hi <name>, a quick heads-up that invoice <number> for <amount> is due on <date>. Here is the invoice and pay link: <link>. If it needs a PO number or should go to someone else, just tell me and I'll resend it. Thanks, <sender>

## 1-7 days overdue
Subject: Invoice <number> - now due
Hi <name>, invoice <number> for <amount> was due on <date>. I've attached it again with the pay link: <link>. Could you confirm when it is scheduled? Thanks, <sender>

## 8-30 days overdue
Subject: Invoice <number> - <n> days past due
Hi <name>, invoice <number> for <amount> is now <n> days past due. Is anything on your side holding it up, such as an approval or a missing detail? If it is scheduled, a payment date would help us close it out. I'll give you a call on <day> in case that's quicker. <sender>

## 31-60 days overdue (recap after a call)
Subject: Invoice <number> - payment date
Hi <name>, thanks for the call today. To confirm: <amount> for invoice <number> will be paid by <method> on <date>. If anything changes, please let me know before then. <sender>

## 61-90 days overdue (from owner or finance lead)
Subject: Invoice <number> - <n> days overdue, action needed
Hi <senior contact>, I'm writing about invoice <number> for <amount>, due <date> and now <n> days overdue despite reminders on <dates>. We value working with <company> and want to resolve this. Please confirm a payment date by <date>, or let me know if a short payment plan would help. <sender, title>

## 90+ days overdue (final notice)
Subject: Final notice - invoice <number>
Hi <name>, invoice <number> for <amount> remains unpaid <n> days after its due date of <date>. Please pay by <date> or contact me to agree a payment plan. If we do not hear from you by then, we will move to the next step described in our agreement. <sender, title>
(Confirm the "next step" with a professional and the contract before sending.)
